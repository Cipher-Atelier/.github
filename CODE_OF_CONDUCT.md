# Community code of conduct

Cipher-Atelier welcomes people who want to learn, test ideas, and improve the evidence together. Expertise can take many forms, and newcomers are welcome.

## What we ask

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree.
- Discuss sources, methods, and claims without personal attacks.
- Credit others’ work and correct mistakes openly.
- Make room for different backgrounds, experience levels, and interpretations.
- Respect privacy, copyright, archival restrictions, and requests to stop unwanted contact.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate misrepresentation of others’ work, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Raising a concern

For an ordinary moderation concern, mention [@cayde-6](https://github.com/cayde-6) in the relevant thread or open a brief issue with a link to it. Share only what is appropriate for a public repository; do not repost private information or harmful material.

For sensitive or abusive content, use GitHub’s built-in reporting and blocking controls rather than putting personal details in a public issue.

## Moderation

The founder and authorized maintainers may request changes, remove harmful content, close a discussion, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Context and severity matter. Membership or past contributions do not exempt anyone from these expectations.

This code applies to Cipher-Atelier repositories, issues, pull requests, and other spaces explicitly maintained for the project.
